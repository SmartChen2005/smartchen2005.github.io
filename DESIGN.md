# Polaroid of Yesterday — Design Context

## Visual thesis
A private game-design archive presented as a sequence of restrained digital exhibition plates: black space, precise typography, low-contrast rules, and the original project imagery treated as evidence.

## Tokens
- Canvas: `#050505`
- Panel: `#0A0B0A`
- Primary text: `#F2F3EF`
- Secondary text: `#9DA19B`
- Dim metadata: `#656963`
- Rules: `#242624`
- Signal accent: `#A58D68` — reserved for chapter numbers, reading progress, and focus.

## Typography
- Display and body: Helvetica Neue / Inter / Arial sans-serif fallbacks.
- Display weight: 200–250.
- Body weight: 300–400.
- Archive metadata: 0.58–0.68rem, uppercase, 0.16–0.24em tracking.
- Body copy: 1rem minimum, controlled to roughly 48rem.

## Layout
- Desktop uses an 18rem fixed archive index and full-viewport reading plates.
- Each plate contains one small unit of the original document.
- Original Notion columns remain image grids; consecutive images remain sequences.
- Mobile removes snap behavior and collapses image grids to one column.

## Signature
The film-counter reader control and two-pixel exposure/progress line translate the Polaroid camera’s finite frame count into document navigation.

## Interaction
- Scroll, chapter links, arrow keys, Page Up/Down, Home/End, and fixed previous/next controls.
- Images open in an accessible native dialog.
- Motion is quiet and disabled under reduced-motion preferences.
- Global scrollbars remain visible and themed.
