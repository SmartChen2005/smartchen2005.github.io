DRIVEFRAME

A dashboard and trip tracker for the joy of driving, from track days to mountain roads.

In the MINI

I built and tested DRIVEFRAME on the Android head unit in my six-speed MINI Cooper S F55. With a compatible Wi-Fi OBD reader, it can also display the telemetry available from other cars.

In this test, a mounted iPhone shared motion and GPS readings over the same local network for trip review on the dash.

Design inspiration

The MINI Cooper is a driver’s car. I wanted the dash to capture the anticipation of a shift and give me a way to relive a run afterward. Motorsport shift lights inspired the row of lamps that fills as the revs rise. Large speed and RPM readouts and a blue G-force target give the rest of the display its rhythm.

After a run, I can scrub one timeline to move through the telemetry traces, G-force plot and GPS route together. I can revisit a corner and see what the car was doing at that moment.
