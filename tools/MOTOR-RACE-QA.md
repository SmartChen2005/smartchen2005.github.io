## Shipped changes

- Street travel averages 30m/s instead of 9m/s, accelerating from 25.5 to 34.5m/s within each shot. Actual integrated distance drives the camera, car positions and wheel rotation. The diagonal pass covers more distance in its original time. Seventy-two seeded peripheral streaks reinforce the city/diagonal footage.
- A separate seventeen-tap, 0.032-second environment shutter reprojects scene depth through analytic camera displacement. It mixes only each shot's three base inks. Vehicles render sharply in a separate depth-tested pass; native text never enters the blur. No previous-frame history is stored, avoiding cut/restart ghosts. Grid/Warp additionally use radial streak extension. Out-of-frame samples fade to ink instead of smearing border pixels into false rectangles.
- Grid Tunnel is a rear chase with both cars; Warp Stars is a frontal sprint with both cars. Roads/buildings remain absent from these abstract shots. Grid flow is four times faster and the star field uses 192 longer streaks. Star tails stop before crossing the camera. The standalone patterns and speed streaks retain deterministic periods dividing twelve seconds; the sixteen-second edited sequence plays once and settles.
- Car shells use a defined shoulder and flatter deck. Tires retain their actual 0.26m width, hollow rims, split spokes, modeled tread and brakes while their rolling radius is about 0.333m. MINI roof height is about 1.42 model units; window frames follow the polygon edges instead of inflated splines. A more upright F56 hatch, restrained LED/projector lamps and recessed rear clusters preserve its identity. The E85 has a lower windshield, smaller roll hoops, long bonnet and retained detailed open cockpit. The brief exhaust flare has a visible bright core.
- Traditional old-town architecture, narrow lanes, city circuit, chequered banners, three-ink palettes, natural solo/following/parallel formations and tachometer linework remain. The distant clock tower uses darker stone so haze does not leave an apparently floating roof.
- The native heading stays in place at z-index 3; only the diagonal MINI silhouette is copied above it at z-index 4. One renderer shares environment, vehicle, shutter and cutout passes. Premultiplied presentation alpha preserves the glass/mask edges, and the foreground canvas is hidden outside this shot. Static city matrices are frozen; dynamic transforms are updated once for all passes. Rendering retains the 1.5 DPR / 2.6M pixel cap and a 1024px shadow map; depth targets avoid multisample resolves and the shutter target is 0.65 resolution. Idle/reduced-motion frames have no shutter displacement or moving streaks.

## Performance and practical limits

Automated Chrome full-sequence presentation counts were low and variable during tuning: a same-machine 2636064 baseline sample was 2.4 presented frames/s and one revised sample was 2.8. An empty RAF control reached 120.6. GPU interval samples for the revised normal stages averaged approximately 2.85ms environment, 1.04ms shutter, 1.97ms vehicles and 0.81ms final composition; the baseline combined scene stage averaged 10.27ms plus 0.64ms printing. The cause of the much slower presentation cadence was not established. These capture samples are not a 60fps or cross-device smoothness guarantee; CPU/static-transform/presentation optimizations were retained and the scene still sleeps when finished, hidden or inactive.

No scene shader/model/controller errors were observed. Existing native `ViewTransition opt-in disabled` aborts can occur when navigating to temporary previews; the shared entrance is unchanged. Safari, physical touch hardware and a full screen-reader audit were not exercised. Git synchronization and actual Pages state are reported separately by the completing chat.
